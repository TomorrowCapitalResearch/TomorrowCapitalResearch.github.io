Context for interpreting the work and illustrations presented here.

Updated 29 October 2026

Illustrations

The stepped growth illustration is an expression of the brand idea: incremental progress and compounding possibilities. It is not market data, a forecast or a return profile. The evaluation timeline illustrates a research process and does not prescribe a particular data split.

Research status

A hypothesis, a historical result, a simulation and an operational outcome represent different kinds of evidence. Public material should not be assumed to be independently audited, peer reviewed, validated or ready for deployment unless the source explicitly establishes that status.

Markets and uncertainty

Models may be affected by selection bias, data leakage, implementation costs or changes in market conditions. Historical and hypothetical outcomes do not guarantee future results. Trading and investment can result in a loss of capital.

Purpose

Nothing on this website is personalised investment advice or an invitation to buy or sell a financial product. The purpose is to explain the research and development work and help readers find the public material.

Earlier material

The earlier conjectures page is a provisional concept archive. Its proposed programme and reward language remain part of that historical concept, rather than a current offer.
