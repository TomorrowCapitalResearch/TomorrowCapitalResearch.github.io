Our purpose is to investigate complex market behaviour with intellectual independence, mathematical discipline and an openness to what the evidence reveals.

01 / OUR IDENTITY

A research-led
institution in the making.

Tomorrow Capital Research is an independent quantitative research initiative focused on systematic trading, financial markets and the mathematical structure of interacting systems.

The ambition is to connect theoretical depth with practical experimentation: to understand a phenomenon, formalise it, test it and, when evidence supports doing so, turn it into a usable system.

02 / PRINCIPLES

How we choose
to think.

01

Intellectual independence

The model must answer to the evidence, not to a narrative or a desired result.

02

Mathematical precision

Clear assumptions, explicit uncertainty and reproducible computational methods.

03

Practical humility

Markets evolve. A tested hypothesis can still fail; careful research acknowledges this openly.

Understanding is a process of reducing uncertainty, not denying it.
Tomorrow / Research philosophy

03 / CONNECTED DISCIPLINES

Beyond a single
discipline.

Probability, statistics, stochastic processes, information theory, dynamical systems, machine learning and market microstructure offer different ways to describe a changing environment.

We explore their intersections without assuming that a useful analogy is a universal law. The value of an idea ultimately depends on what can be derived, measured and reproduced.
