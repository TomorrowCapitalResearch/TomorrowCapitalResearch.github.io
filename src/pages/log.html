02

What the log says so far

Size is predictable. Direction mostly is not.

Shocks cluster like aftershocks, volatility persists and quiet volume comes before quiet markets, on every coin tested. Directional effects are small: 0.05σ on an hourly BTC bar is roughly 2 to 3 bps, against costs of 9 bps per side. We read them as states for a market maker (when to quote, how wide, which side) rather than as entries for a taker.

Familiar chart rules mostly fail, and several reverse.

Price levels as magnets, trends defined by rising highs and lows, record-high reversals and range breakouts show nothing. Runs of closes and path extremes tend to give back rather than continue.

Nothing is shown to be tradable yet.

Sign patterns are worth about 0.4 bps per trade at zero cost and disappear with a 0.5 bp offset. At 9 bps per side almost no cross-asset rule stays positive in both halves of the history. The log exists to find out which ideas survive that test.
