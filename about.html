What we do

We investigate how systems behave, how decisions can be improved, and how an idea becomes something that works.

Our work brings together quantitative research, statistical modelling and software engineering. Financial markets are a central focus: their interactions, uncertainty and changing conditions provide difficult questions worth studying.

The same curiosity extends to complex systems more broadly, including the relationship between individual behaviour and collective outcomes.
