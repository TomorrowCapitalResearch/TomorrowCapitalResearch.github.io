No investment advice

Nothing on this website constitutes investment, legal, tax or other professional advice, nor an invitation or offer to buy or sell securities, currencies, digital assets or financial products.

Research and simulation

Figures, visualisations, examples and analytical descriptions may be theoretical, illustrative, simulated or historical. The wave-field and equilibrium illustrations on this site are conceptual models, not observed market data or forecasts.

Investment and trading risk

Markets involve uncertainty. Trading and investment positions can lose value, including the entire capital committed. Market liquidity, volatility, operational failure and execution costs may materially affect outcomes.

Performance and validation

Hypothetical or historical results are not reliable guarantees of future returns. Models can be overfit, biased by selection or data leakage, and vulnerable to changes in market structure and participant behaviour.

Status of material

Public documents should not be assumed to be independently audited, peer reviewed, production ready or validated unless explicitly stated with appropriate supporting evidence. See the research page for related material.
